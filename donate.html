Link Fort Bend is entirely volunteer-run — every donation goes directly toward connecting more volunteers with the local organizations that need them.

Where it goes

What your donation supports.

We keep overhead as low as possible. Contributions help cover the basics that let us keep matching volunteers to opportunities across Fort Bend County:

Printed materials

Flyers, walk lists, door hangers, and yard signs for canvassing and registration events.

Events & trainings

Space rental and supplies for volunteer orientations, meetings, and community events.

Website & tools

Hosting, sign-up forms, and the tools that keep volunteer matching running smoothly.

Volunteer support

Basic supplies so volunteering doesn't cost our volunteers out of pocket.
